One Punch Man

Superhero, Action, Comedy

follows Saitama, an incredibly powerful superhero who can defeat any opponent with a single punch.

One Punch Man: Road to Hero (OVA, 2015)

A prequel OVA showing Saitama before he became overwhelmingly strong, training hard and struggling to be noticed as a hero.

Season 1 (2015)

Saitama joins the Hero Association and is paired with the cyborg Genos, taking on threats no other hero can handle with a single punch.

OVA Collection (2015-2019)

A set of bonus side-story episodes following Saitama and the other heroes between the main season storylines.

Season 2 (2019)

The Monster Association rises as Garou, the Hero Hunter, begins targeting heroes one by one, pushing Saitama into the growing conflict.

Season 3 (2025)

The Monster Association's assault escalates as the Hero Association's strongest heroes clash with monsters threatening to level the city.
