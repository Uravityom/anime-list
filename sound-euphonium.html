Sound! Euphonium

Music, Drama , School

follows Kumiko Oumae, a girl seeking a fresh start in high school. Despite swearing off music after a middle school competition trauma, she is reluctantly drawn into the Kitauji High School Concert Band. Under a new, strict advisor, Kumiko and her bandmates must overcome personal struggles and teenage drama to pursue their ultimate goal of reaching the national brass band competition.

Sound! Euphonium: Season 1 (2015)

Kumiko reluctantly joins the Kitauji High School Concert Band and watches it transform from a lax club into a serious competitor under a new advisor.

Ready, Set, Monaka (OVA, 2016)

The band members who didn't make the competition lineup form "Team Monaka" and find their own way to support Kumiko and the others before the big contest.

Welcome to the Kitauji High School Concert Band (Movie, 2016)

A recap film retelling the events of Season 1 from Kumiko's first days in the band through its hard-fought climb to competition.

Sound! Euphonium: Season 2 (2016)

As Kumiko becomes a second-year, the band faces new internal conflicts and rivalries while pushing for consecutive national competition wins.

Our Promise: A Brand New Day (Movie, 2016)

A recap film covering Season 2's story, following the band's continued growth and Kumiko's deepening bonds with her bandmates.

Liz and the Blue Bird (Movie, 2018)

A spin-off film focusing on oboist Mizore Yoroizuka and flutist Nozomi Kasaki, exploring their fragile friendship through a shared solo piece.

Sound! Euphonium: Season 3 (2024)

Kumiko returns as a college student mentoring the next generation of Kitauji bandmates as they chase their own national competition dream.

Our Promise: Ensemble Contest (Movie, 2024)

The Kitauji band members form smaller ensembles to compete in a contest that tests their teamwork outside the full concert band setting.

Sound! Euphonium the Movie: Finale (Movie, 2024)

The story reaches its emotional conclusion as Kumiko and her bandmates perform one last time before graduating from Kitauji High School.
