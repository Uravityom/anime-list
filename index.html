ANIME LIST I'VE WATCHED

Pokémon

Action-Adventure, Comedy, Fantasy

Action, Sports, Shonen

My Hero Academia

Action, Superhero, Comedy

Action, Supernatural, Dark Fantasy

Tokyo Revengers

Action, Drama, School

Demon Slayer

Action, Supernatural, Dark Fantasy

Follows Tanjiro Kamado, who joins a secret corps of swordsmen to avenge his murdered family and cure his sister, Nezuko, after she is turned into a demon.

Assasination Classroom

Action, Comedy, School

Naruto

Boruto: Naruto Next Generations

Action, Adventure, Fantasy

follows Naruto's son, Boruto Uzumaki, as he forges his own path as a ninja in a modern, peaceful era.

Tokyo Ghoul

Action, Dark Fantasy, Horror

Sword Art Online

Action, Adventure, Sci-Fi

Follows 10,000 gamers who are trapped inside a hyper-realistic Virtual Reality MMORPG.

Attack On Titan

Action, Drama, Fantasy

humanity surviving inside cities surrounded by massive walls to protect themselves from man-eating, giant humanoids called Titans.

One Piece

Action, Adventure, Comedy

A young pirate who ate a mystical Devil Fruit that turned his body into rubber. Inspired by his childhood hero, he sets out to sail the treacherous Grand Line, gather a unique crew, and claim the legendary "One Piece" treasure to become the next Pirate King.

Re:Zero - Starting Life in Another World

Action, Dark Fantasy, Horror

Subaru Natsuki, an ordinary shut-in suddenly transported to a fantasy world.

My Dress Up Darling

Romance, Comedy, School

Wakana Gojo, a socially anxious high schooler who makes traditional Japanese dolls, and Marin Kitagawa, a popular, outgoing girl.

Bleach

Action, Supernatural, School

Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who gains the supernatural powers of a Soul Reaper after accidentally absorbing them from Rukia Kuchiki.

Akebi's Sailor Uniform

Slice of Life, Comedy, School

When she is accepted into the prestigious all-girls Roubai Academy—her mother’s alma mater—she is thrilled to wear the handmade sailor suit. However, she discovers the school has switched to blazers, though the headmistress happily makes an exception just for her.

Fire Force

Action, Comedy, Supernatural

Shinra Kusakabe, a young pyrokinetic with the ability to ignite his feet at will. He joins Company 8 to put Infernals to rest and uncover the truth about the mysterious fire that killed his family years ago.

That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime

Action, Comedy, Fantasy

Satoru Mikami, a 37-year-old corporate worker, is fatally stabbed while protecting a colleague. Reincarnated in a fantasy world as a blind, goopy slime, he gains the "Predator" skill to absorb anything and "Great Sage" to compute decisions.

Spy X Family

Slice of Life, Comedy, School

follows Agent Twilight, a master spy who must build a fake family to infiltrate a prestigious school and investigate an elusive political leader.

Shikimori's Not Just a Cutie

Romance, Comedy, School

follows the daily life of high school sweethearts Yuu Izumi and Miyako Shikimori.

Black Clover

Action, Fantasy, Magic

Two orphans, Asta and Yuno, raised in a world where magic determines one's worth.

Hunter X Hunter

Actiov, Adventure, Fantasy

Gon Freecss as he leaves his home to become a "Hunter"—an elite, licensed adventurer.

My Stepmom's Daugther Is My Ex

Romance, Comedy, Slice of Life

middle-school exes Mizuto and Yume become step-siblings after their parents remarry. To protect their parents' happiness, they agree to a strict "sibling rule": whoever gets attracted to the other first loses, leading to ongoing romantic tension.

One Punch Man

Superhero, Action, Comedy

follows Saitama, an incredibly powerful superhero who can defeat any opponent with a single punch.

Chainsaw Man

Action, Comedy, Horror

Denji, an impoverished teen who merges with his pet devil, Pochita, to become a chainsaw-wielding hybrid.

Record Of Ragnarok

Action, Shonen, Fantasy

a fantasy battle series where the gods decide to annihilate humanity after 7 million years of existence.

Blue Lock

Sports, Shonen, Psychological

A controversial coach named Jinpachi Ego isolates 300 of the country's best young strikers in a prison-like facility, forcing them into intense competitions to forge the ultimate, egoistic goal-scorer.

Dead Mount Death Play

Action, Dark Fantasy, Mystery

a powerful necromancer called the Corpse God reincarnates into modern-day Shinjuku, Japan, after swapping souls with a murdered teenager named Polka Shinoyama.

RWBY: Ice Queendom

Science, Fantasy, Action

a group of teenage girls train at Beacon Academy to become "Huntresses." They are tasked with protecting their civilization from demonic monsters known as the Grimm and thwarting the plots of an immortal witch named Salem.

Overlord

Isekai, Fantasy, Supernatural

a veteran player named Momonga decides to stay in his favorite dive-MMO, Yggdrasil, as its servers permanently shut down. Instead of disconnecting, he is trapped inside the game world in the form of his skeletal avatar.

Vinland Saga

Action, Adventure, Historical

a historical epic following Thorfinn, a boy raised by Vikings in 11th-century Europe. After witnessing his father's murder, Thorfinn is consumed by vengeance, joining his enemy's mercenary band.

Solo Leveling

Action, Fantasy, Adventure

humanity’s weakest E-rank Hunter. After a brutal double-dungeon trap nearly kills his party, he is chosen by a mysterious program called "the System" to become a "Player". Unlike others, he can infinitely increase his power, eventually becoming the all-powerful Shadow Monarch.

Wind Breaker

Action, Delinquent, School

Haruka Sakura is the new lonely first-year high school student at Furin High School, which is recognized in town as a school for delinquents with its students' notoriously bad academic reputation.

Kaiju No.8

Action, Shonen, Sci-Fi

32-year-old Kafka Hibino works as a cleanup crew member clearing monster remains. After a parasitic creature enters his body, he gains the ability to transform into a humanoid monster known as "Kaiju No. 8".

Dandadan

Horror, Comedy , Action

follows Momo Ayase, a spirit medium, and Ken "Okarun" Takakura, an occult geek. After a bet to prove their conflicting beliefs, they accidentally unleash supernatural powers and curses.

Sound! Euphonium

Music, Drama , School

follows Kumiko Oumae, a girl seeking a fresh start in high school. Despite swearing off music after a middle school competition trauma, she is reluctantly drawn into the Kitauji High School Concert Band. Under a new, strict advisor, Kumiko and her bandmates must overcome personal struggles and teenage drama to pursue their ultimate goal of reaching the national brass band competition.

86 Eight Six

Military, Sci-Fi, Mecha

follows the Republic of San Magnolia, which claims its war against the Giadian Empire is bloodless because it uses "unmanned" drones.

Violet Evergarden

Drama, Fantasy, Tragedy

follows an emotionally detached former child soldier who takes a job as an "Auto Memory Doll" (ghostwriter) to reintegrate into society.

Sakamoto Days

Action, Comedy, Shonen

Taro Sakamoto, once the world's most feared hitman, retires after falling in love, marrying, and gaining weight. Now a chubby, humble convenience store owner, his peaceful life is constantly threatened by underworld assassins and a massive bounty, forcing him to protect his family while strictly keeping his promise never to kill.

Mashle: Magic and Muscles

Fantasy, Shonen, Comedy

follows Mash Burnedead, a young man born without a trace of magic. To protect his peaceful life with his adoptive father, Mash enrolls in a prestigious magic academy.

Frieren: Beyond Journey's End

Fantasy, Adventure, Drama

Follows the elven mage Frieren as she journeys on after the death of the hero party she once traveled with, confronting the slow passage of time and forming new bonds along the way.
