Pokémon

Action-Adventure, Comedy, Fantasy

10-year-old Ash Ketchum from Pallet Town as he sets out to become a Pokémon Master, traveling across regions with his partner Pikachu, catching and training Pokémon, battling Gym Leaders, and facing rivals and villainous teams along the way.

Pokémon: Indigo League

The season follows Ash Ketchum, a 10-year-old Pokémon Trainer and aspiring Pokémon Master from Pallet Town as he receives a Pikachu from Professor Oak and travels across the Kanto region to challenge its Pokémon League while joined by Misty and Brock.

Pokémon: Mewto Strikes Back(1998)

follows a bio-engineered, hyper-powerful psychic Pokémon named Mewtwo who rebels against his creators and plots to prove his superiority over humanity and natural Pokémon.

Pokémon: Adventures in the Orange Islands

The season follows Ash Ketchum as he travels across the Orange Islands to challenge its Pokémon League while joined by Misty and Tracey Sketchit, a Pokémon Watcher.

Pokémon: The Power of One(1999)

follows Ash Ketchum as he tries to stop a global climate disaster caused by a collector capturing the legendary bird Pokémon. 

Pokémon: The Johto Journeys

The season follows Ash Ketchum as he travels across the Johto region to challenge its Pokémon League while joined by Misty and Brock.

Pokémon: Spell of the Unown(2000)

follows young Ash Ketchum as he battles an illusionary Entei and powerful Unown entities to rescue his mother from a crystallized fantasy world.

Pokémon: Johto League Champions

The season follows Ash Ketchum as he continues travelling across the Johto region with Misty and Brock.

Pokémon: 4Ever(2001)

Celebi is chased through time by a villain. Ash meets a boy named Sammy in the past and helps Celebi.

Pokémon: Master Quest

The season follows Ash Ketchum as he continues travelling across the Johto region with Misty and Brock.

Pokémon: Heroes(2002)

In the city of Alto Mare, Latios and Latias guard a secret treasure called the Soul Dew. Thieves try to steal it and the heroes step in.

Pokémon: Advanced

Ash arrives in Hoenn with May, Max, and Brock. May wants to be a Coordinator.

Pokémon: Jirachi Wish Maker(2003)

Jirachi wakes up once every 1,000 years to grant wishes. Max becomes its friend. The group has to stop a man who wants to use its power for evil.

Pokémon: Advanced Challenge

More Hoenn badges and contests, with a tougher rivalry.

Pokémon: Destiny Deoxys(2004)

Deoxys crashes to Earth and ends up in LaRousse City. Rayquaza comes to fight it, and Ash and friends try to protect the city.

Pokémon: Advanced Battle

Ash goes for the Hoenn League.

Pokémon: Lucario and the Mystery of Mew(2005)

Lucario is stuck inside a staff for hundreds of years. Ash meets him and learns the story of Sir Aaron and Mew.

Pokémon: Battle Frontier

Ash takes on the Battle Frontier in Kanto. He faces the Frontier Brains in tough battles.

Pokémon: The Ranger and the Temple of the Sea(2006)

Manaphy is stolen by a pirate group. Ash and friends team up with a Pokémon Ranger to take Manaphy back to the Temple of the Sea.

Pokémon: Diamond and Pearl

Ash arrives in Sinnoh with Dawn and Brock. Dawn enters contests, and Ash goes for the gyms.

Pokémon: The Rise of Darkrai(2007)

Dialga and Palkia fight in Alamos Town. Darkrai is blamed for the chaos. Ash and friends find out what's really going on.

Pokémon: Battle Dimension

More gym fights and contests. Team Galactic starts to cause trouble.

Pokémon: Giratina and the Sky Warrior(2008)

Giratina is hunting Shaymin and drags a villain into its world. Ash helps Shaymin reach the Gracidea flowers so it can change form.

Pokémon: Galactic Battles

Ash and friends stop Team Galactic's plans.

Pokémon: Arceus and the Jewel of Life(2009)

A man named Damos tries to take revenge on Arceus, the creator Pokémon. Ash ends up in the middle, and the fate of Alamos Town is at stake.

Pokémon: Sinnoh League Victors

Ash goes through the Sinnoh League, and Dawn goes for the Grand Festival.

Pokémon: Zoroark Master of Illussions(2010)

Zoroark uses illusions to protect its cub from a man who wants to use it. Ash and friends in Crown City try to help.

Pokémon: Black & White

Ash arrives in Unova with Iris and Cilan, and meets all-new Pokémon.

Pokémon: Victini and Reshiram/Zekrom(2011)

A Victini story is told in two versions. One features Reshiram and the other Zekrom, the legendary dragons of Unova.

Pokémon: Black & White Rival Destinies

Ash faces strong rivals and the Unova League.

Pokémon: Kyurem vs The Sword of Justice(2012)

Keldeo is training to become a Sword of Justice. Kyurem, a powerful ice dragon, is its enemy.

Pokémon: Adventures in Unova and Beyond

The gang travels to other places. They see new regions and finish the Unova story.

Pokémon: Genesect and the Legend Awakened(2013)

Genesect, a Pokémon changed by Team Plasma, is angry and dangerous. A legendary Mewtwo steps in to help.

Pokémon: X&Y

Ash arrives in Kalos with Clemont, Bonnie, and Serena. Ash and Greninja form a special bond.

Pokémon: Diancie and the Cocoon of Destruction(2014)

Diancie is a diamond Pokémon. Ash and friends help protect it from a greedy villain. Xerneas and Yveltal are part of the story.

Pokémon: X&Y: Kalos Quest

Ash goes for the Kalos gyms. Team Flare's plan begins to take shape.

Pokémon: Hoopa and the Clash of Ages(2015)

Hoopa has a powerful side that is sealed away. Ash and friends help it learn to control its power.

Pokémon: X&Y: XYZ

Ash stops Team Flare and goes for the Kalos League finals.

Pokémon: Volcanion and the Mechanical Marvel(2016)

Volcanion and a robot-like Pokémon named Magearna are chased by a man who wants Magearna's power. Ash helps them.

Pokémon: Sun & Moon

Ash goes to school in Alola and meets new friends. He learns about Z-Moves and island challenges.

Pokémon: I Choose You!(2017)

A retelling of Ash's early journey with Pikachu. It follows their first meeting, and Ash chasing Ho-Oh.

Pokémon: Sun & Moon Ultra Adventures

Ultra Beasts and Ultra Wormholes show up, and Team Skull causes problems.

Pokémon: The Power of Us(2019)

A wind festival in Fula City is thrown into danger when a legendary Pokémon shows up. A group of people with different goals have to work together.

Pokémon: Sun & Moon Ultra Legends

Ash enters the first Alola League and wins it.

Pokémon: Mewto Strikes Back(2019)

A 3D remake of the first movie, with the same story.

Pokémon: Journeys

Ash meets Goh and becomes a research fellow. They travel across many regions.

Pokémon: Secrets of the Jungle(2020)

A boy named Coco was raised by Pokémon in the jungle. He thinks he's a Pokémon, and Ash meets him and learns his story.
