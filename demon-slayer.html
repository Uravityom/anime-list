Demon Slayer

Action, Supernatural, Dark Fantasy

Follows Tanjiro Kamado, who joins a secret corps of swordsmen to avenge his murdered family and cure his sister, Nezuko, after she is turned into a demon.

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba (Season 1, 2019)

Tanjiro joins the Demon Slayer Corps and trains to become strong enough to protect his sister Nezuko and avenge his family.

Demon Slayer: Mugen Train Arc (2021)

Tanjiro and his friends join Flame Hashira Kyojuro Rengoku on a mission aboard the Mugen Train, where they face the demon Enmu.

Demon Slayer: Entertainment District Arc (2021-2022)

Tanjiro teams up with Sound Hashira Tengen Uzui to investigate demons hiding in the Entertainment District.

Demon Slayer: Swordsmith Village Arc (2023)

Tanjiro receives a new sword in the Swordsmith Village and faces off against two of the terrifying Upper Moon demons.
