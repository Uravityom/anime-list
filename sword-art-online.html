Sword Art Online

Action, Adventure, Sci-Fi

Follows 10,000 gamers who are trapped inside a hyper-realistic Virtual Reality MMORPG.

1. Sword Art Online: Season 1 (2012)

Kirito is trapped in the deadly VR game Aincrad with Asuna, then fights through ALfheim Online to rescue her. Covers the Aincrad and Fairy Dance arcs.

2. Sword Art Online: Extra Edition (Special, 2013)

A recap of Season 1 wrapped around a new story where Kirito and friends hunt for a rare whale in an underwater ALO quest.

3. Sword Art Online II (2014)

Kirito enters Gun Gale Online to investigate Death Gun alongside Sinon, followed by the Calibur quest and Asuna's story with Yuuki in Mother's Rosario.

4. Sword Art Online the Movie: Ordinal Scale (2017)

A new augmented-reality game sweeps Japan, but Kirito grows suspicious when players start losing their memories of Sword Art Online.

5. SAO Alternative: Gun Gale Online (Spin-off, 2018)

A side story set in GGO following Llenn, a tall girl who plays as a tiny pink-clad avatar, as she competes in the Squad Jam battle royale.

6. Sword Art Online: Alicization (2018-2019)

Kirito wakes up in the Underworld, a vast VR world with its own laws, and befriends Eugeo and Alice while uncovering a sinister experiment.

7. Alicization: War of Underworld (2019-2020)

The Underworld faces an invasion from the real world as Kirito's friends rally to defend it in a final war that decides its fate.

8. Progressive: Aria of a Starless Night (Movie, 2021)

A retelling of Kirito and Asuna's early days on Aincrad's first floors, told from Asuna's point of view.
