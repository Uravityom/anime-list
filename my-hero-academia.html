My Hero Academia

Action, Superhero, Comedy

In a world where 80% of humanity possesses superpowers called "Quirks", Quirkless teenager Izuku Midoriya dreams of becoming a superhero.

My Hero Academia: Season 1 (2016)

Izuku Midoriya inherits the power of One For All from All Might and enrolls at U.A. High School to begin his training as a hero.

My Hero Academia: Save! Rescue Training!(OVA)

Takes place four weeks after the Unforeseen Simulation Joint (U.S.J.) attack.

My Hero Academia: Season 2 (2017)

Class 1-A competes in the U.A. Sports Festival before facing the villain Stain during their hero work-studies.

My Hero Academia: Training of the Dead(OVA)

Class 1-A joins a survival training camp with four transfer students from Isamu Academy High School: Romero Fujimi, Habuko Mongoose, Kashiko Sekigai, and Dadan Tadan.

My Hero Academia: Two Heroes (Movie, 2018)

Izuku and All Might travel to a floating man-made island for I-Island's hero expo, where a villain's plot puts everyone at risk.

My Hero Academia: Season 3 (2018)

The League of Villains kidnaps Bakugo, leading to an all-out rescue and a training camp arc that tests Class 1-A's resolve.

My Hero Academia: Make It! Do-or-Die Survival Training!(OVA)

follows Class 1-A as they face a dangerous disaster simulation set in an underground shopping mall ahead of their Provisional Hero License Exam.

My Hero Academia: Season 4 (2019)

Class 1-A battles the Shie Hassaikai in Kamino as Izuku and Shoto try to rescue Eri from Overhaul's grasp.

My Hero Academia: Heroes Rising (Movie, 2019)

Class 1-A's remedial island training turns deadly when the villain Nine attacks, forcing the students to protect the island on their own.

My Hero Academia: Season 5 (2021)

Class 1-A takes on the Joint Training Arc against Class 1-B as the Meta Liberation Army and League of Villains begin to merge.

My Hero Academia: World Heroes' Mission (Movie, 2021)

A radical group called Humarize launches a worldwide terror plot, forcing young heroes to team up and stop them before it's too late.

My Hero Academia: Season 6 (2022)

The Paranormal Liberation War erupts as heroes and villains clash in an all-out battle that reshapes society and claims major losses on both sides.

My Hero Academia: Season 7 (2023-2024)

In the war's aftermath, Izuku goes on the run alone to protect his friends while the final battle against All For One and Shigaraki draws near.
