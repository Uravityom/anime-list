Jujutsu Kaisen

Action, Supernatural, Dark Fantasy

follows high schooler Yuji Itadori, who is thrust into a secret world of sorcery after eating one of the severed fingers of Ryomen Sukuna, a legendary demon.

Jujutsu Kaisen: Season 1 (2020-2021)

Yuji Itadori enrolls at Tokyo Jujutsu High and trains alongside Megumi and Nobara to master his cursed power and hunt down Sukuna's remaining fingers.

Jujutsu Kaisen 0 (Movie, 2021)

A prequel following Yuta Okkotsu, a timid student haunted by the cursed spirit of his childhood friend Rika, as he learns to control his curse at Jujutsu High.

Jujutsu Kaisen: Season 2 (2023)

Covers Gojo's past in the Hidden Inventory arc and the catastrophic Shibuya Incident, where sorcerers and curses clash in an all-out war.
